### 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a **Full-Stack Developer** from São Paulo, Brazil 🇧🇷, who sees coding not as a job, but as the ultimate puzzle—as engaging as a video game and as satisfying as a crossword. I genuinely love what I do.

My passion for building things extends far beyond the digital world. When I'm not at the keyboard, you'll likely find me:

*   🔧 **Immersed in Mechanics:** I have a deep curiosity for how things work, which often leads me to disassemble and reassemble my car and motorcycle engines just for the thrill of understanding them.
*   🔩 **Designing & 3D Printing:** This hands-on approach with mechanics inspired me to dive into 3D modeling and printing, where I design and create custom parts for my vehicles.
*   🌍 **Exploring Geopolitics:** I'm fascinated by the dynamics that shape our world.
*   ✈️ **Dreaming of Flight:** My ultimate dream is to one day own and fly my own airplane.

I believe this blend of digital craftsmanship and mechanical engineering gives me a unique perspective on problem-solving.
